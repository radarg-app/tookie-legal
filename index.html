Everything Tookie does

The app’s features, one by one, and which are free and which come with Tookie+.

9:41

Log an expense in seconds

Free and Tookie+

Tap +, type the amount on the app’s keypad and pick a category. Income works the same way, and every transaction can be in any of 38 currencies.

  • When you save, you see how much you’ve spent in that category this month.
  • Expenses shows every transaction, day by day: swipe a row to edit or delete it.
  • Free plan: 15 expenses and income entries a week, renewed every Monday. With Tookie+, unlimited.
9:41

Scan receipts with AI

Free and Tookie+

Snap the receipt, or pick a screenshot of an online purchase from your gallery: the built-in AI receipt scanner reads the store, the amount, the date and the category, and you check it before saving.

  • Each detail shows whether it was found (a check) or not (a cross).
  • The photo is only used to read the receipt and isn’t stored by Tookie.
  • Free plan: 5 scans a month. With Tookie+, unlimited.
9:41

Before you buy, see what it really costs

Tookie+

“Should I buy it?” is a Tookie+ tool: enter the price and see how many hours of work it costs, what that money could be worth invested and how much of your goal it would cover.

  • “I won’t buy it” adds what you didn’t spend to Money saved or to a goal.
  • “I’ll think about it tomorrow” asks you the next day if you still want it.
  • Hours are based on the salary you enter in Profile → Salary.
9:41

Put the stores that tempt you on pause

Tookie+

With Shopping block, a Tookie+ feature, you choose the shopping apps you’d rather avoid on impulse. If you try to open one, Tookie reminds you of the decision you made.

  • A single button, “I’ll wait”, and a count of how many times you chose to wait this week.
  • To change your mind you go back to Tookie: the block screen can’t unlock the store.
  • It uses Apple’s Screen Time, and Tookie never sees which apps you have.
9:41

Budgets that warn you ahead of time

Tookie+

With Tookie+ you decide how much you want to spend in each category, in money or in number of purchases, and Tookie warns you before you go over.

  • If a month gets tight, raise it just for that month or move money from another category.
  • After 60 days of use, it compares each month with your usual, even if you went over.
9:41

Savings goals and net worth

Free and Tookie+

Give what you want a name and an amount, and see how much you still need. Each goal moves forward with your contributions or with what’s left over each month.

  • Goals on the free plan: 1. With Tookie+, as many as you want.
  • With Tookie+, the Net worth tab adds up your savings, investments and debts: what you own minus what you owe.
  • Each debt shows how much you’ve paid off.

Weekly review and monthly wrap-up

Free

Once a week, Tookie asks about a few of your purchases: if you could choose again, would you make it again? From the 1st to the 7th of each month, it tells you how last month went.

  • Up to 5 purchases a week, and you always decide what counts.
  • The monthly wrap-up shows one thing that went well and just one thing to try.

Frequently asked questions

Tookie at a glance

Tookie is a personal finance app for iPhone that helps you track your spending and save money. On the free plan you log up to 15 expenses and income entries a week, by hand or with a photo of the receipt, which AI reads for you, and set up your first savings goal. With Tookie+, the paid plan, you add budgets by category, “Should I buy it?” (how many hours of work a purchase costs you), Shopping block, fixed costs and installments that log themselves, and your net worth. Its features are inspired by behavioral science studies, and Nara, the app’s helper, keeps you company along the way.

What it is
A personal finance app to track your expenses and income, set budgets, save for your goals and see your net worth.
Who it’s for
People who want to know where their money goes, buy less on impulse and save for something specific, without spreadsheets.
Platforms
iPhone (iOS).

Is Tookie free?

Yes, Tookie is free to start. On the free plan you log up to 15 expenses and income entries a week, use your history and filters, scan 5 receipts a month and set up your first savings goal. Tookie+ is the paid plan, with the rest of the tools.

What does Tookie+ include and how much is it?

Tookie+ costs US$29.99 a year or US$5.99 a month. If it’s your first time with Tookie+, both plans start with a 3-day free trial. It adds unlimited expenses, income and scans, budgets, “Should I buy it?”, Shopping block, fixed costs and installments, your net worth, unlimited goals and categories, iPhone widgets, and achievements and accessories for Nara. Your store shows the price in your currency.

How do I cancel Tookie+?

You can cancel anytime from the store where you bought it. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or “Manage subscription” inside Tookie. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you already paid for, and if you cancel at least 24 hours before the free trial ends, you won’t be charged.

Does Tookie connect to my bank?

No. Tookie doesn’t connect to your bank, your cards or digital wallets, and it never asks for their passwords. You add every transaction yourself: by hand, in seconds, or with a photo of the receipt.

How does receipt scanning work?

You snap the receipt, or pick a screenshot of an online purchase from your gallery, and AI reads the store, the amount, the date and the category. Before saving you check every detail and can change it; if the receipt has no readable date, today’s date is used. The free plan includes 5 scans a month, and Tookie+ has no limit.

What happens to the receipt photo?

The photo is only used to read the receipt and is not stored, either in your account or on Tookie’s servers. It travels encrypted to Tookie’s server, which passes it to OpenAI to read it: if the receipt shows your personal details, such as your name, OpenAI can see them. What’s kept is the expense you confirm and a record of the scan with what was read, without the photo. The details are in the Privacy policy.

What happens to my data?

Your data is stored so the app works and it’s there when you sign in again, including from another phone. Tookie doesn’t sell your data, doesn’t show ads and doesn’t track you across other apps or websites. Everything travels encrypted, and one account can’t read another’s data. More in the Privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete account. It’s permanent: your account and your data are erased. Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription, so if you pay for Tookie+, cancel it in the store first.

Which phones does Tookie work on?

Tookie is an iPhone app, and you get it from the App Store.

Which languages and currencies does it support?

Tookie is available in 16 languages: Spanish, US and UK English, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Swedish, Norwegian, Danish, Turkish, Simplified Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Each expense keeps its own currency, out of 38, and totals are converted to your main currency.

What is Shopping block?

It’s a Tookie+ feature that puts the shopping apps you choose on pause. If you try to open one, a Tookie screen appears with a single button, “I’ll wait”, and to open it again you have to change your decision in Tookie. It uses Apple’s Screen Time, and Tookie never sees which apps you have. It depends on your iPhone’s permissions and may not be available on every device.

Who is Nara?

Nara is Tookie’s helper. She keeps you company throughout the app, her face changes with how your month is going, she celebrates your achievements and she walks you through it in the Tookie tour. Your first expense earns her a hoodie, on any plan. With Tookie+ you can dress her up all the way: 16 accessories are ready to wear and the rest you earn with achievements.

How is Tookie different from other expense trackers?

Tookie is designed to help you spend less, not just to keep records. Before you buy, it shows how many hours of work something costs and lets you put the stores that tempt you on pause; its features are inspired by behavioral science studies; it doesn’t connect to your bank, so you decide what goes in; and it doesn’t scold you: your streak gets a rest day and Nara keeps you company.
